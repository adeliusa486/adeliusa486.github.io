Hi, I'm Adeel — working at the intersection of cybersecurity, AI, and networks.

I'm a final-year Information Technology student at IUM, studying on a fully-funded Saudi Government scholarship awarded for academic excellence. My journey into computing began in Gujranwala, Pakistan, with a simple curiosity — taking apart small programs and networks to understand how they really work — and has grown into a focused mission: making artificial intelligence secure, private, and explainable enough to protect the networks the world depends on. Over the past two years I've co-authored peer-reviewed research on privacy in federated learning, designed interpretable intrusion-detection systems for software-defined networks, and built complete systems from Raspberry Pi hardware to cloud dashboards. I'm currently sharpening my practical security skills through hands-on TryHackMe cybersecurity labs, alongside my AWS and networking training. I care deeply about research that is rigorous, reproducible, and genuinely useful — and I'm ready to bring that curiosity and drive to a research team advancing trustworthy AI for critical networks.
